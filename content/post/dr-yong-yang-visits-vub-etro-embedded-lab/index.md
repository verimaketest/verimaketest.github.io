Dr. Yong Yang visited the VUB ETRO Embedded Lab and met with Bruno da Silva for an exchange on embedded systems research, education, and future collaboration opportunities.

During the visit, they held preliminary discussions on establishing a joint RISC-V laboratory between WCH and ETRO. The proposed collaboration aims to connect industrial RISC-V platforms with ETRO's research and teaching activities in embedded computing, creating opportunities for joint experimentation, student training, and applied hardware-software innovation.
