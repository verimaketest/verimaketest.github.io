On May 28, 2026, Bruno da Silva visited the Electrical and Electronic Experiment Center at Southeast University at the invitation of Prof. Ming Ling, where he joined a dedicated education and teaching exchange seminar.

Prof. Ming Ling, director of the Electrical and Electronic Experiment Center, together with graduate student representatives from the School of Integrated Circuits, participated in the exchange and lab visit. Prof. Ling first introduced the center's history, laboratory development, experimental course offerings, and recent teaching reform activities.

Bruno da Silva then introduced Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB), with a focus on the research directions, training programs, and recent research outcomes of the Electronics and Informatics Department (ETRO). During the open discussion, both sides explored potential collaboration in embedded-systems education, talent development, and scientific research.

Building on this visit, the two sides aim to further deepen teaching and research exchanges, continue exploring new models for innovative engineering education, and support the steady improvement of higher engineering education quality.
