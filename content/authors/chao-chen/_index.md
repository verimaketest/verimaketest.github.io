Chao Chen received his Ph.D. degree from Southeast University, Nanjing, China, in 2024. In 2021, he joined Vrije Universiteit Brussel as a Visiting Student. He is currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the School of Microelectronics, Fudan University, Shanghai, China. His research interests focus on domain-specific computing architectures, particularly in accelerating biomedical signal processing on FPGAs. He also has a strong interest in developing wearable algorithms and hardware.
