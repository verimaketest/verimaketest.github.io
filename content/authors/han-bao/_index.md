Han Bao was born in China in 2000. In 2022, he obtained the Bachelor of Engineering degree in Microelectronics from Northwestern Polytechnical University, China. In 2023, he obtained the Master of Science Degree in Electrical Engineering from Vrije Universiteit Brussel and Université Libre de Bruxelles (Bruface program). Later that year he joined Electronics and Informatics (ETRO) department of the Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB) as a PhD student. His research topic is FPGA based applied edge AI.
